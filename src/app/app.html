Help for coaches

Choose a tab above to prepare and present your lesson.

  1. Choose material: PDF Library opens the website's worksheets. Open local file lets you use a PDF or image from your device.
  2. Present a position: click a highlighted board to enlarge it. Use Full screen when presenting to the class.
  3. Move through the lesson: use ← and → for pages in page view, or diagrams in the enlarged view. Press Esc to return to the page.
  4. Play a video: choose Video Library, then Watch on YouTube. The video opens in another tab; return here to continue your lesson.
